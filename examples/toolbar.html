--- layout: example.html title: Toolbar Control shortdesc: Group controls in toolbars. docs: > This example uses the ol/control/Toolbar control to group other controls. The default zoom and rotate controls are left out of the map and added to toolbars instead: a vertical toolbar in the upper left corner holds the zoom controls, a horizontal toolbar in the upper right corner holds the rotate and full screen controls. A third, horizontal toolbar at the bottom holds custom controls to fly to a few places. Use the position and orientation options to place a toolbar, and addControl to add controls to it. A fourth, external, vertical toolbar, below the map, shows that a toolbar can also be placed outside the map, with the target option. It then becomes part of the page layout, without any additional CSS. tags: "toolbar, control, custom, zoom, rotate, fullscreen" ---
